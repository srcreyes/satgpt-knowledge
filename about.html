About SatGPT

A next-generation spatial decision support system developed by UN ESCAP Space Applications Section, integrating AI and Earth observation for disaster risk reduction.

UN ESCAP Space Applications Section

Innovative geospatial applications powered by the fourth industrial revolution (4IR) technologies, such as artificial intelligence (AI), Internet of Things (IoT), cloud computing and big data analytics, are making critical contributions to advance progress toward the Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs). Such innovative applications are introduced and scaled up through field and technical cooperation projects on topics such as agricultural resilience and food security, air pollution monitoring and crop biodiversity, amongst others.

ESCAP actively strives to integrate geospatial applications across multiple sectors and create more opportunities to support the achievement of the SDGs through its field projects, capacity-building activities, regional cooperative mechanisms and innovative geospatial applications.

Developed by the space applications team in the secretariat, SatGPT is a prime example of an innovative geospatial tool that exemplifies cutting-edge technological innovation in disaster management. SatGPT integrates large language models (LLMs) with cloud computing platforms and Earth observation data to form a comprehensive spatial decision support system. It enhances the speed, accessibility and real-time capabilities of flood mapping, and simplifies advanced geospatial analyses, thereby enabling community leaders, policymakers and disaster response teams to make informed decisions. SatGPT received the ESCAP Innovation Award for 2024 and has been shortlisted for the SDG Digital GameChangers Award under the Planet category.

Learn more at unescap.org/our-work/ict-disaster-risk-reduction
