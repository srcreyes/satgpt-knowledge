Online Courses

Self-paced courses on Earth observation, AI, and geospatial tools for flood mapping and disaster risk reduction.

Introduction to Google Earth Engine and ChatGPT for Flood Mapping
Learn to harness the power of Google Earth Engine's cloud computing platform alongside ChatGPT to map flood extents using Sentinel-1 SAR data. This course guides you through processing large-scale Earth observation datasets and using AI assistance for geospatial analysis.

Leveraging Large Language Models for Big Earth Data Processing: Mapping Flood Hotspots and Impacts
Explore how Large Language Models can be integrated with satellite data pipelines to automate flood hotspot identification and impact assessment. This advanced course covers prompt engineering for geospatial tasks, automated code generation, and multi-source data fusion.
